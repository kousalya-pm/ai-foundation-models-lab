# Incident Response Policy

**Synthetic Acme Corporation policy created for this experiment -- not a real company, not real data.**

**Version: 2026-01-01 (current)**

## Severity: Critical

Critical incidents require immediate escalation to the incident commander, with no review delay permitted.

## Severity: High

High severity incidents must be reviewed by an analyst within 30 minutes of detection.

## Containment Logging

All containment actions, whether manual or automated, require audit logging including the acting analyst or system, timestamp, and justification.
