# Asset Criticality Classification

**SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT -- for local RAG learning purposes only. This is entirely fictional and does not represent any real organization's policy.**

## Criticality Tiers

Critical: domain controllers, certificate authorities, and core identity infrastructure.

High: production servers, production databases, and customer-facing application servers.

Medium: developer laptops and internal engineering workstations.

Low: test systems, staging environments, and sandbox VMs.

## Response Time Expectations

Critical asset incidents must begin containment within 15 minutes of confirmed compromise.

High asset incidents must begin containment within 1 hour of confirmed compromise.

Medium asset incidents must begin containment within 4 hours of confirmed compromise.

Low asset incidents must begin containment within 1 business day of confirmed compromise.
