# Endpoint Response Policy

**SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT -- for local RAG learning purposes only. This is entirely fictional and does not represent any real organization's policy.**

## When Isolation Is Allowed

Endpoint isolation is allowed without prior approval only during a confirmed active ransomware outbreak.

In all other cases, isolation requires approval from a Tier 2 or higher analyst.

## Evidence Required Before Containment

Before isolating a non-ransomware endpoint, the analyst must document at least two of the following: a malicious process, an unauthorized outbound connection, a known-bad file hash, or a confirmed exploited vulnerability.

Isolating an endpoint solely on the basis of a single low-confidence antivirus alert is not permitted without additional corroborating evidence.

## Approval Chain

Approval requests must go through the on-call Tier 2 or Tier 3 analyst, or the SOC Manager. Approval given verbally must be documented in the case ticket within one hour.
